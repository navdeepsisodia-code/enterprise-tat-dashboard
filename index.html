How to read a row

Meeting TAT means every SKU that can already be judged finished inside the promise. A SKU still younger than the promise does not fail the enterprise. Missed TAT means at least one SKU finished after the promise, or has no QC-done record and is already older than the promise. No new SKUs means nothing was created in this window. Typical finish is the median time of the SKUs that did reach QC-done. Click a bar to filter the table.
