How to read a row

“133 days past TAT” means one SKU was still waiting, and it was already 133 days beyond the promised TAT, when the cron last saved the row. It is not the time since the rooftop last did any QC. “12 h still inside TAT” means that oldest waiting SKU was still within its TAT. “No SKU waiting” means a row was saved and the unassigned queue was empty. “Not measured” means there is no saved row.
